# Study Area & Data

**Burnie, Tasmania · 56 SA1 areas · Coastal urban and inland rural landscapes**

## Study Area: Burnie, Tasmania

Burnie is a regional coastal city in north-west Tasmania, Australia, along Bass Strait. Its coastal urban area and inland rural landscapes provide contrasting settlement patterns for examining accessibility.

The study covers **56 Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) Statistical Areas Level 1 (SA1s)**, which form the spatial units of analysis.

We compare access within **30 minutes** by **private car and public bus** to six categories of essential services and everyday opportunities: **schools, healthcare facilities, pharmacies, supermarkets, greenspaces and leisure facilities**.

Access to these destinations influences residents’ ability to participate in everyday activities. Comparing the two transport modes helps identify areas of greater car dependency, particularly where limited bus accessibility coincides with **socio-economic disadvantage**.

## Data Supporting the Analysis

The study combines **spatial, public transport and census datasets** to connect Burnie’s transport network, essential services and community characteristics. The datasets support travel-time modelling, interpretation of commuting patterns and assessment of accessibility equity.

| Dataset | Source | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SA1 and municipal boundaries** | ABS, 2021 | Define the Burnie study area and its 56 spatial analysis units |
| **Cadastral parcels** | LIST Tasmania | Support study-area delineation and spatial clipping |
| **Road network** | OpenStreetMap | Model car travel times and walking connections to public transport |
| **Bus stops, routes and timetables** | Metro Tasmania GTFS | Represent stop locations, scheduled services and service frequencies for public bus travel-time modelling |
| **Commuting patterns** | ABS Census, 2021 | Describe travel to work by private vehicle and public bus |
| **Schools** | Prepared school-location dataset | Identify education destinations |
| **Healthcare facilities** | LIST community facilities | Identify healthcare destinations, including hospitals and dental facilities |
| **Community facilities and other points of interest** | LIST Tasmania | Provide context on community amenities and essential-service locations |
| **Pharmacies and supermarkets** | OpenStreetMap | Identify medicine and grocery destinations |
| **Greenspaces and leisure facilities** | OpenStreetMap | Identify recreation destinations and everyday opportunities |
| **Population** | ABS, 2021 | Describe the population associated with each SA1 |
| **Socio-economic disadvantage** | ABS SEIFA, 2021 | Examine accessibility equity using the Index of Relative Socio-economic Disadvantage (IRSD) |
| **Satellite basemap** | ESRI | Provide geographic context for the study-area map |
| **Tasmania locator boundary** | LIST Map| Locate Burnie within Tasmania |
